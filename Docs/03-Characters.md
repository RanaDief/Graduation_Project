# Characters

## Overview

The main characters are:

- The Protagonist
- The Father
- The Mother

The Father also appears as a psychological representation inside the protagonist's coma world.

---

# 1. The Protagonist

## Role

**Main Character / Player Character**

The protagonist is a young man who becomes trapped in a coma after a car accident.

The player experiences the entire story through his perspective.

## Personality

At the beginning of the game, he is:

- Frustrated.
- Emotionally overwhelmed.
- Defensive.
- Angry with his father.
- Convinced that his father does not understand him.

As the story progresses, he becomes more reflective and begins questioning his own understanding of the conflict.

## Character Arc

His emotional journey is:

**Anger → Guilt → Understanding → Acceptance**

He eventually recognizes that:

- His father genuinely cared about him.
- His father struggled to communicate that care.
- He also contributed to the conflict.
- The past cannot be changed.
- He can still choose how to move forward.

## Gameplay Role

The protagonist:

- Explores the environments.
- Inspects objects.
- Solves puzzles.
- Reconstructs memories.
- Experiences Flashbacks.
- Makes emotional choices.
- Manages Emotional Pressure.

---

# 2. The Father

## Role

**Major Story Character**

The father is the central figure in the protagonist's emotional conflict.

He is not presented as completely good or bad.

He genuinely cares about his son but struggles to communicate his concerns effectively.

His attempts to provide guidance and protection are often perceived by the protagonist as pressure and control.

## Personality

The father is:

- Responsible.
- Hard-working.
- Protective.
- Concerned about his family.
- Emotionally reserved.
- Sometimes controlling.
- Not good at expressing his feelings.

## Character Arc

The father's character is primarily revealed through the protagonist's memories.

At first, the protagonist remembers him mainly as the source of pressure and conflict.

Later, he discovers:

- His father's work responsibilities.
- His financial concerns.
- His responsibility toward the family.
- His concern for his son.
- His attempts to help.

This allows the protagonist to see his father from a more complete perspective.

## Psychological Representation

Inside the coma world, the father appears as a psychological representation created from the protagonist's memories.

This version of the father acts as a guide through the protagonist's emotional confrontation.

He does not provide direct answers.

Instead, he asks questions and challenges the protagonist's understanding of what happened.

---

# 3. The Mother

## Role

**Supporting Character**

The mother has a smaller role during the psychological world and becomes important during the ending.

She represents the family connection that remains after the conflict.

## Personality

The mother is:

- Caring.
- Supportive.
- Concerned about her son.
- Emotionally aware.
- Protective of the family.

She is not heavily involved in the main conflict between the protagonist and his father.

## Gameplay / Story Role

The mother mainly appears through:

- Family memories.
- Family photographs.
- Environmental storytelling.
- The final hospital scene.

At the end of the game, she is present when the protagonist wakes up and apologizes to his parents.
