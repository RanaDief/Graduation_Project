# Level 2 — The Last Evening

## Overview

Level 2 continues the player's exploration of his memories after leaving the hospital.

The player enters a distorted version of his family home. The environment is familiar, but the house contains fragments of the evening that happened before the accident.

The player discovers that the memory of that evening is incomplete. Important parts of the argument are scattered throughout the house.

The main objective of the level is to reconstruct the sequence of events that led to the player leaving the house.

The level establishes:

- The argument with the player's father happened shortly before the accident.
- The argument developed gradually rather than happening all at once.
- The player remembers fragments of the conversation but not the complete sequence.
- The player's memories are distorted and incomplete.
- The player can reconstruct memories by connecting environmental evidence with memory fragments.
- The player's understanding of the events is still incomplete.

---

# Level Flow

## Phase 1 — Entering the House

### Starting State

The player enters the family living room through the door at the end of Level 1.

The room resembles the player's home.

At first, the environment appears normal.

The player can explore the room.

The environment contains:

- Sofa.
- Television.
- Dining table.
- Chairs.
- Family photographs.
- Wall clock.
- Phone.
- Personal belongings.
- Door leading to the kitchen.
- Door leading to the player's bedroom.
- Main entrance door.

There are no people inside the house.

Several objects appear slightly out of place.

The television is turned off.

The wall clock is working normally.

The room is quiet.

### Player Reaction

The player recognizes the room.

Possible dialogue:

> "Home..."

The player begins exploring.

---

# Phase 1 — Exploration

The player can freely explore the living room.

Several objects provide small pieces of information about the family and the player's life.

The player eventually discovers the first unusual object: a memory fragment.

---

## Interaction 1 — Memory Fragment: The Entrance

The player discovers a distorted visual fragment near the main entrance.

The fragment resembles a broken piece of the house.

When the player interacts with it, a short image appears:

The front door opens.

Someone enters the house.

The image immediately disappears.

The player cannot see the person's face clearly.

The fragment is incomplete.

The player cannot place it yet.

---

## Interaction 2 — Father's Work Bag

The player notices a work bag near the entrance.

The bag appears to belong to the father.

It contains ordinary work-related belongings.

The player interacts with it.

A short memory fragment plays.

The player sees the father entering the house and placing the bag down.

The memory ends.

This provides evidence that the first memory fragment belongs near the entrance.

---

## Interaction 3 — Family Photograph

The player discovers a family photograph.

The photograph shows the player with his parents during an earlier positive moment.

When the player interacts with it, the photograph briefly becomes clear.

A short positive memory plays.

The family is together at home.

They are laughing and talking.

The memory lasts several seconds before disappearing.

The photograph becomes distorted again.

Possible dialogue:

> "We used to be happy..."

The memory provides emotional context for the family relationship but does not directly advance the main puzzle.

---

# Memory Echoes

After the player explores the living room, several environmental Memory Echoes begin appearing.

Memory Echoes are fragments of past events embedded within the environment.

They do not directly increase Emotional Pressure.

Their purpose is to:

- Reveal fragments of the argument.
- Provide environmental clues.
- Establish the relationship between the characters.
- Help the player reconstruct the evening.

Memory Echoes are triggered by interacting with specific objects or entering specific areas.

---

## Echo 1 — Television

The player approaches the television.

The screen suddenly turns on.

Static fills the screen.

A distorted voice plays:

> "We need to talk."

The television turns off.

The room returns to normal.

---

## Echo 2 — Phone

The player approaches the phone.

The phone vibrates even though there is no incoming call.

The player interacts with it.

A short voice fragment plays:

> "You've been avoiding this."

The phone screen turns black.

---

## Echo 3 — Kitchen Door

When the player approaches the kitchen door, voices can be heard from the other side.

The voices are distorted.

The player hears:

> "Just listen..."

Then:

> "You never listen to me!"

The voices disappear.

The kitchen door becomes accessible.

---

# Phase 1 Completion

After the player has explored the living room and discovered the initial memory fragments and Memory Echoes, the player enters the kitchen.

The kitchen contains the next part of the memory.

The environment is initially normal.

The player can explore the kitchen and discover the second memory fragment.

---

# Phase 2 — The Kitchen Memory

## Interaction 1 — Kitchen Table

The player discovers a memory fragment near the kitchen table.

The fragment shows two people sitting at the table.

The conversation is incomplete.

The player hears:

> "I'm trying to help you."

The memory cuts out.

The player cannot determine the complete context yet.

---

## Interaction 2 — Two Cups

Two cups are sitting on the kitchen table.

One cup is partially empty.

The other is untouched.

When the player interacts with them, a short memory plays.

The player sees the father and son sitting at the table.

The father attempts to start a conversation.

The boy avoids eye contact.

The memory ends before the argument begins.

This establishes that the kitchen was where the serious conversation began.

---

## Interaction 3 — Phone Messages

The player investigates the phone again.

A message is visible:

> "Can we talk when you get home?"

The message has been read but not answered.

This provides additional context for the conversation.

---

# Phase 2 Completion

After investigating the kitchen, the player has discovered enough information to begin reconstructing the evening.

The player now has two memory fragments:

- The father arriving home.
- The conversation beginning in the kitchen.

The player must determine where each fragment belongs in the house.

The main reconstruction puzzle becomes available.

---

# Phase 3 — Memory Reconstruction Puzzle

The player discovers several distorted memory fragments throughout the house.

Each fragment represents a different part of the evening.

The player must determine where each fragment belongs based on environmental evidence.

The player is not given the correct order directly.

The environment provides clues that allow the player to determine the correct location.

---

# Memory Fragment 1 — Father Arrives

### Evidence

The player's father is carrying his work bag.

The entrance contains the same work bag in the present environment.

### Correct Location

**Main Entrance**

### Memory

The father enters the house.

He places his work bag down.

He looks toward the living room.

The memory ends.

---

# Memory Fragment 2 — Conversation Begins

### Evidence

The fragment contains the kitchen table and two cups.

The same cups are present in the kitchen.

### Correct Location

**Kitchen**

### Memory

The father and son sit at the kitchen table.

The father begins a conversation.

> "We need to talk."

The boy remains quiet.

The memory ends.

---

# Memory Fragment 3 — Argument Escalates

### Evidence

The fragment contains the boy's headphones.

The headphones are located in the boy's bedroom.

### Correct Location

**Boy's Bedroom**

### Memory

The boy enters his bedroom.

He picks up his headphones.

The argument can still be heard from outside.

The boy puts the headphones on.

The memory ends.

---

# Memory Fragment 4 — Boy Leaves

### Evidence

The fragment contains the main entrance door.

The door is shown open.

The boy is carrying his belongings.

### Correct Location

**Main Entrance**

### Memory

The boy walks toward the front door.

The father remains behind him.

The boy opens the door.

The memory ends before the accident.

---

# Puzzle Mechanics

The player places each memory fragment at the location where the event occurred.

The player uses environmental evidence to determine the correct location.

### Correct Reconstruction

Father Arrives
      ↓
Conversation Begins
      ↓
Argument Escalates
      ↓
Boy Leaves
