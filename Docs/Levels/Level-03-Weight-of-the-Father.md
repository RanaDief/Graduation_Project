# Level 3 — The Weight of the Father

## Overview

Level 3 explores the father's perspective.

After reconstructing the final evening, the boy begins questioning his memories of his father.

The entire level takes place inside a distorted version of the father's office.

Everything required to complete the level is contained within the office.

The player discovers evidence of the responsibilities and pressure his father was dealing with at the time.

The goal is not to prove that the father was right.

Instead, the player begins understanding that the father was dealing with problems that the boy never knew about.

The level establishes:

- The father had responsibilities outside the family.
- The father was under pressure.
- His behavior came partly from fear and concern.
- His intentions and his actions were not always the same.
- Understanding his father does not erase the hurt caused by the argument.

---

# Level Flow

## Phase 1 — The Father's Office

### Starting State

The player enters a distorted version of the father's office.

Unlike Level 2, the space feels personal and isolated.

The room contains everything needed to understand the father's perspective and complete the level.

The player hears a faint ticking clock.

Possible dialogue:

> "I never really looked at this stuff..."

The player begins exploring.

---

# Phase 1 — Exploration

Important objects include:

- Father's work desk
- Work documents
- Bills
- Family photographs
- Work tools
- Wall clock
- Father's jacket
- Car keys
- Telephone
- Computer
- Notebook
- Filing cabinet
- Personal belongings connected to the son

The objects reveal information gradually.

They do not directly explain the father's perspective.

The player must piece the information together through exploration.

---

## Interaction 1 — Work Desk

The player discovers his father's work desk.

Several documents are scattered across it.

Some contain unfinished work.

Others contain notes and deadlines.

The player interacts with the desk.

The sound of papers being moved plays.

A short memory begins.

### Memory

The father sits at the desk late at night.

He looks tired.

He checks the time and continues working.

The memory fades.

Possible dialogue:

> "He was still working..."

---

## Interaction 2 — Bills

The player finds several unpaid bills.

The exact details are unclear, but it is obvious that his father was dealing with financial responsibilities.

The player interacts with them.

A short memory plays.

The father looks through the bills before putting them away.

He takes a deep breath.

The memory ends.

Possible dialogue:

> "I didn't know about this."

---

## Interaction 3 — Father's Jacket

The father's jacket is sitting on the office chair.

The player interacts with it.

A short memory begins.

The father arrives home after work.

He enters the office, removes his jacket, and drops into the chair, exhausted.

The memory fades.

Possible dialogue:

> "He looked exhausted..."

---

## Interaction 4 — Family Photograph

The player discovers an older family photograph inside the office.

It shows the boy and his father during a positive memory.

The father is spending time with him.

The memory briefly becomes real.

The boy remembers a moment when his father supported him.

The memory fades.

Possible dialogue:

> "He was always there..."

# Memory Echoes

Memory Echoes appear throughout the father's office.

They reveal small pieces of the father's life.

They do not directly increase Emotional Pressure.

They provide:

- Additional context
- Small memories
- Information about the father's responsibilities
- Positive memories between the father and son

---

## Echo 1 — Clock

The wall clock begins ticking loudly.

The sound becomes distorted.

A short memory shows the father checking the time repeatedly while working.

The echo disappears.

---

## Echo 2 — Telephone

The telephone rings.

The father answers in the memory.

The conversation cannot be clearly heard.

The father says:

> "I'll take care of it."

The call ends.

The echo disappears.

---

## Echo 3 — Old Photograph

The player finds another photograph.

The image shows the father spending time with his son when he was younger.

The memory briefly becomes clear.

The boy and his father laugh together.

The memory fades.

Possible dialogue:

# Phase 1 Completion

After investigating the important objects, the player returns to the father's desk.

The objects begin shifting slightly.

The room becomes darker.

The ticking clock becomes louder.

The player hears several fragments of the father's voice.

The voices overlap:

> "I have to..."

> "I'm trying..."

> "I just want..."

The room becomes completely silent.

Distorted memory fragments begin appearing around the office.

The main puzzle begins.

---

# Phase 2 — Understanding the Father's Burden

The player discovers several memory fragments connected to the father's responsibilities.

The fragments are not inventory items.

They appear as distorted pieces of reality containing brief visual or audio glimpses from the past.

The player must identify what each fragment represents and align it with the correct location inside the office.

Environmental clues help the player determine the correct locations.

The fragments represent:

1. Work
2. Financial responsibility
3. Family responsibility
4. Concern for his son

---

# Memory Fragment Mechanic

The player:

1. Discovers a memory fragment.
2. Inspects it.
3. Examines the surrounding office for clues.
4. Approaches the location that matches the memory.
5. The fragment reacts when the player is close.
6. The player interacts with the location.
7. The fragment aligns with the environment.
8. The office temporarily transforms into the memory.
9. A short memory plays.
10. The office returns to its distorted state.

The player is reconstructing memories rather than collecting objects.

---

## Memory Fragment 1 — Work

The player finds a fragment showing the father working late.

The work documents provide the clue.

**Work Documents = Work = Father's Desk**

### Correct Alignment

The fragment reacts when brought near the father's desk.

The player interacts with the desk.

The fragment aligns with the desk and the environment transforms into the memory.

The father sits at the desk working late.

He checks the time.

He continues working despite being tired.

The memory ends.

---

## Memory Fragment 2 — Financial Responsibility

The player finds a fragment showing the father dealing with bills.

The bills provide the clue.

**Bills = Financial Responsibility = Father's Desk**

### Correct Alignment

The fragment reacts near the desk.

The player aligns it with the desk.

The father looks through the bills.

He appears worried.

He puts them away and continues working.

The memory ends.

---

## Memory Fragment 3 — Family Responsibility

The player finds a fragment connected to the father's relationship with his family.

The family photographs provide the clue.

**Family Photograph = Family = Photo Area**

### Correct Alignment

The fragment reacts near the family photographs.

The player aligns it with the photograph area.

The father sits with his family.

The boy is younger.

They share a simple family moment.

The memory ends.

---

## Memory Fragment 4 — Concern for His Son

The final fragment shows the father thinking about his son.

The son's photograph or personal belongings provide the clue.

**Son's Belongings = Concern for Son = Father's Desk / Personal Area**

### Correct Alignment

The fragment reacts when the player approaches the relevant personal object.

The player aligns the fragment with the object.

The father looks at a photograph or personal belonging connected to his son.

He pauses before returning to his work.

The memory ends.

---

# Incorrect Placement

If the player aligns a fragment with the wrong location:

- The room becomes darker.
- The clock begins ticking faster.
- The father's voice becomes distorted.
- A short distorted memory fragment plays.
- The fragment separates and resets.
- Emotional Pressure increases slightly.

The distorted memory can provide additional context.

The player can use the information to reconsider the correct location.

Normal experimentation should not be heavily punished.

---

# Phase 2 Completion

After all fragments are correctly aligned, the room becomes quiet.

The memories begin connecting.

The player sees:

- His father working.
- His father dealing with bills.
- His father spending time with the family.
- His father worrying about his son.

The player begins understanding that his father was carrying responsibilities that he never saw.

However, the memories do not portray the father as completely innocent.

The final memory begins.

---

# Phase 3 — The Father's Perspective

The player sees a memory from the night of the argument.

The father is alone before the conversation begins.

He looks at the clock.

He sees the boy.

He hesitates.

The father approaches him.

The conversation begins.

The memory shows the same events from a slightly different perspective.

The father is worried about his son.

However, his concern comes across as pressure.

He tries to give advice.

The boy becomes frustrated.

The father becomes defensive.

The argument escalates.

The memory ends before the boy leaves.

---

# The Realization

The player returns to the distorted office.

The boy reflects on what he has seen.

Possible dialogue:

> "He was worried..."

Pause.

> "But he never knew how to say it."

The player realizes that his father's intentions and actions were not always the same.

His father may have wanted to protect him, but the way he communicated created pressure and hurt.

The boy also realizes that his own interpretation of the argument was incomplete.

Understanding his father does not mean that everything his father did was correct.

It means the boy can finally see the situation from more than one perspective.

---

# Emotional Pressure

Emotional Pressure continues from previous levels.

Memory Echoes do not directly increase Pressure.

Pressure increases mainly through:

- Incorrect memory placements
- Intense memories
- Extended exposure to distorted memories
- The final argument

### Low Pressure

- Stable environment
- Clear audio
- Normal lighting

### Medium Pressure

- Slight visual distortion
- Louder breathing
- Increased ticking sounds
- Lighting flickers

### High Pressure

- Strong environmental distortion
- Overlapping voices
- Louder breathing
- Distorted memories

The player should still be able to complete the level without heavy punishment.

---

# Level Ending

After the final memory, the office becomes silent.

The father's belongings begin fading away.

The room becomes empty.

A family photograph remains on the desk.

The player picks it up.

For a moment, the photograph becomes clear.

The boy sees himself and his father together.

The memory fades.

The player places the photograph back.

A doorway appears inside the office.

Behind it is a distorted corridor leading deeper into the boy's memories.

The player walks toward it.

The screen fades to black.

**Level 3 Complete.**
