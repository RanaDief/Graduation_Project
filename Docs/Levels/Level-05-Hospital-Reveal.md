# Level 5 — The Wake Up

## Overview

Level 5 is the final level and emotional conclusion of the game.

After confronting his memories, understanding his father's perspective, and accepting that he cannot change what happened, the boy is finally ready to leave the psychological world.

The level reveals the truth of the real world:

- The boy is the one who has been in a coma.
- His father survived the accident.
- His father has been beside him while he was unconscious.
- The hospital world the player experienced was created by the boy's mind.
- The memories were not meant to change the past.
- They allowed the boy to understand what happened and come to terms with it.

The main purpose of the level is to provide emotional closure.

The player does not need to solve another major puzzle.

Instead, the final challenge is simply waking up and facing the people he was afraid of facing before the accident.

The level ends with the boy finally speaking honestly to his parents.

---

# Level Flow

## Phase 1 — Darkness

The level begins immediately after the end of Level 4.

The screen is completely black.

There is almost complete silence.

A faint hospital monitor can be heard in the distance.

**Beep.**

Silence.

**Beep.**

The sound becomes slightly clearer.

The player hears breathing.

At first, it sounds distant.

Then it becomes more recognizable.

The boy hears his father's voice.

> "Come on..."

Pause.

The voice becomes clearer.

> "Wake up."

The screen remains black.

The player hears the hospital monitor again.

**Beep.**

The sound becomes stronger.

The player's breathing becomes noticeable.

The screen slowly begins to brighten.

---

# Phase 2 — The Real Hospital

The screen fades in.

The player is no longer inside the psychological hospital.

He is lying in an actual hospital bed.

The environment is completely different from the surreal hospital seen throughout the game.

The room is:

- Quiet.
- Naturally lit.
- Realistic.
- Slightly messy.
- Clearly occupied.

There are medical machines beside the bed.

A heart monitor is connected to the player.

The player slowly opens his eyes.

His vision is blurry.

He tries to move.

His hand moves slightly.

The heart monitor reacts.

The player hears someone breathing nearby.

He slowly turns his head.

His father is sitting beside the bed.

His father looks exhausted.

He has clearly been there for a long time.

The father notices the movement.

He immediately looks toward his son.

For a moment, neither of them says anything.

The father stands up.

Possible dialogue:

> "You're awake..."

The boy looks at him.

He is confused and emotional.

Possible dialogue:

> "Dad..."

The father approaches the bed.

He does not immediately touch him.

There is a short silence.

The boy looks around the room.

He realizes that this is the real hospital.

---

# Phase 3 — Understanding Reality

The boy looks toward the medical equipment.

He sees the hospital bracelet on his wrist.

Unlike the damaged bracelet from the psychological world, this one is real.

He looks at his hands.

He remembers the accident.

Then the memories from the psychological world begin flashing through his mind.

Short flashes appear:

- The family photograph.
- The frozen clock.
- The argument.
- The father's office.
- His father's work.
- The bills.
- The family photograph.
- The conversation with his father.
- The road.
- The accident.

The memories are no longer distorted.

They appear clearly.

The boy understands what happened.

He was never walking through a real hospital.

He was inside his own mind.

The entire psychological world was built from his memories, fears, guilt, and regret.

Possible dialogue:

> "It wasn't real..."

Pause.

> "But I remember."

The father looks at him.

> "What do you remember?"

The boy does not immediately answer.

He looks down.

---

# Phase 4 — The Father Is Alive

The boy looks at his father again.

This is the moment where the player realizes that the father survived.

The father sits beside him.

The boy notices that his father looks tired and worried.

There is no supernatural distortion.

No strange environment.

No psychological effects.

Just his father.

Possible dialogue:

> "You stayed?"

The father responds:

> "Of course I stayed."

The boy looks away.

He remembers the final moments before the accident.

His father's voice:

> "Wait."

His own voice:

> "Just leave me alone!"

The boy becomes emotional.

He realizes that his last words to his father before the accident were angry ones.

---

# Phase 5 — Talking to His Parents

The boy's mother enters the room.

She has been waiting outside.

When she sees that he is awake, she immediately approaches the bed.

She holds his hand.

The boy looks at both of his parents.

For the first time since the argument, he is able to speak to them without anger.

The conversation is simple and emotional.

The boy first speaks to his father.

> "I'm sorry."

His father remains silent.

The boy continues.

> "I shouldn't have left like that."

Pause.

> "I was angry."

The father responds:

> "I know."

The boy looks down.

> "I thought you didn't understand me."

His father answers:

> "Maybe I didn't."

A short silence.

> "But I was trying."

The boy begins to cry.

He looks toward his mother.

> "I'm sorry."

His mother holds his hand.

She tells him:

> "You're here now."

The boy realizes that he still has a chance to talk to his parents.

The argument did not have to be the final moment of their relationship.

---

# Phase 6 — The Apology

The boy looks at his father.

This is the emotional climax of the game.

He finally says what he was unable to say before the accident.

Possible dialogue:

> "I'm sorry for what I said."

Pause.

> "I was hurt."

> "But I hurt you too."

His father looks at him.

The father does not immediately respond.

He reaches toward his son and holds his hand.

Possible dialogue:

> "You don't have to fix everything right now."

The boy nods.

The mother remains beside them.

The three of them stay together.

There is no dramatic speech.

The scene is intentionally quiet.

The important thing is that they are finally together and talking.

---

# Phase 7 — The Second Chance

The boy looks toward the hospital window.

The outside world is bright.

For the first time in the game, there is no distortion.

No flickering lights.

No distorted voices.

No strange hospital corridors.

No pressure.

Everything is normal.

The boy closes his eyes for a moment.

He remembers the psychological world.

The memories of his father.

The good moments.

The argument.

The accident.

The conversation.

He understands that he cannot change any of them.

But he can choose what happens next.

Possible internal dialogue:

> "I can't change what happened."

Pause.

> "But I can choose what happens now."

The camera slowly moves away from the hospital bed.

The boy remains with his parents.

---

# Phase 8 — Final Scene

The hospital room becomes quiet.

The father remains sitting beside the bed.

The mother is standing nearby.

The boy looks at both of them.

He gives a small smile.

His father asks:

> "You okay?"

The boy answers:

> "Yeah."

Pause.

> "I'm just glad you're here."

His father smiles.

The mother holds his hand.

The camera slowly moves toward the window.

Outside, the world continues normally.

Cars pass.

People walk.

The sun shines through the window.

The hospital monitor continues beeping steadily.

The screen slowly fades to black.

---

# Final Line

Before the screen completely fades out, the boy says:

> "I want to go home."

The screen goes completely black.

A final hospital monitor beep is heard.

**Beep.**

Silence.
