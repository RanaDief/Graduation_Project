# Collectables

## Overview

Collectables are optional objects that the player can discover and interact with while exploring the game's environments.

They provide an additional reason to explore areas beyond the main objectives without requiring a separate side-mission or quest system.

Most collectables are ordinary personal objects connected to the player's life, family, childhood, or memories.

Some collectables may have a small story connection, while a limited number can trigger a Memory Echo.

The system is intentionally simple and reusable so it can be implemented by a three-person team.

---

## Purpose

Collectables are used to:

- Encourage exploration.
- Reward players for investigating the environment.
- Provide additional story and emotional context.
- Make environments feel more personal and lived-in.
- Connect the surreal environments to the player's memories.
- Give players optional content without requiring side missions.

Collectables should remain secondary to the main gameplay and story.

The player should be able to complete the game without finding every collectable.

---

## Collectable Types

Collectables are divided into three simple categories.

### 1. Ordinary Objects

These are optional objects that primarily reward exploration.

Examples:

- Old pen
- Coffee mug
- Key
- School notebook
- Movie ticket
- Old book
- Childhood toy
- Old phone

When collected, they are added to the collection.

They may have a short description but do not trigger additional gameplay.

---

### 2. Story-Related Objects

These objects have a stronger connection to the player's life or family.

Examples:

- Family photograph
- Birthday card
- Father's watch
- Father's old jacket
- Childhood drawing
- Old school certificate
- Family recipe
- Personal letter

When collected, they provide a short description that gives additional context about the player's memories.

They do not create a separate mission.

---

### 3. Memory Objects

These are special collectables that can trigger a short Memory Echo when inspected.

Examples:

- Old family photograph
- Childhood toy
- Father's phone
- Old headphones
- Personal bracelet
- Object connected to the accident

The object is collected after the player interacts with it, and a short Memory Echo can play.

The Memory Echo follows the existing Memory Echo system.
